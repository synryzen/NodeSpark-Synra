# Code Of Conduct

NodeSpark Synra contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, focused, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about bugs and risks.
- Keep technical discussion grounded in reproducible behavior, code, logs, screenshots, or design goals.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask people to share secrets, private logs, raw camera frames, raw audio, or face samples publicly.
- Help keep Synra safe around smart-home actions, local memory, camera, microphone, and NodeSparkHub pairing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, tokens, logs, images, or personal data.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to bypass safety, privacy, or confirmation flows.
- Repeated off-topic comments that make issues or pull requests harder to resolve.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; request changes; or block participation when needed to protect the project and contributors.
