# Synra Asset Policy

Synra's character assets are product identity, not stock art.

Protected assets include:

- Synra Classic and any other Synra avatar or character design;
- `.vrm`, `.vrma`, Live2D, motion, rigging, texture, background, icon, and UI
  art files;
- screenshots and marketing images showing Synra;
- voice/personality prompts, assistant identity text, and branded response
  behavior;
- generated cels, concept art, rigging sheets, and production handoff files.

Do not extract, copy, resell, rebrand, redistribute, train on, or use these
assets in another app, assistant, avatar, service, dataset, model, or product
without written permission.

Contributors may submit improvements to the original repository, but submitting
an asset does not authorize third parties to reuse Synra's identity outside the
project.
