# Trademarks And Brand Use

Synra, NodeSpark, NodeSparkHub, Synryzen, the Synra character identity, app
icons, logos, product screenshots, trade dress, and related visual branding are
owned by Matthew C Elliott unless otherwise stated.

## Allowed References

You may use the names `Synra`, `NodeSpark`, or `NodeSparkHub` only to accurately
refer to the original project, app, compatibility target, or integration. For
example:

- "Compatible with NodeSparkHub."
- "Bug report for NodeSpark Synra."
- "Integration notes for Synra Standalone."

## Not Allowed Without Written Permission

You may not:

- name a product, app, assistant, avatar, service, repo, marketplace listing, or
  downloadable package `Synra`, `NodeSpark`, `NodeSparkHub`, or a confusingly
  similar name;
- use Synra's character design, hair, outfit, face, screenshots, avatar style,
  logo, icon, or visual identity to market another product;
- imply that a fork, clone, integration, device image, hosted service, or
  derivative assistant is official;
- sell or redistribute Synra-branded assets, avatar files, motion files,
  screenshots, prompts, voice/personality behavior, or UI art;
- register domains, social handles, app names, trademarks, package names, or
  store listings that are likely to confuse users about source or ownership.

## Forks And Contributions

Forks and pull requests should preserve attribution and should not present
themselves as official releases. If you build an integration, use your own
product name and make the relationship clear.

## Reporting Misuse

Report confusing copies, app-store clones, asset theft, or brand misuse to the
repository owner with links, screenshots, dates, and any storefront or account
details.
